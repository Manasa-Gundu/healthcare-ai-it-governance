Healthcare Quality & IT Governance Analytics
Data Observations

Dataset:
CMS Hospital General Information

Initial observation:
The Hospital overall rating field contains ratings from 1 to 5, as well as "Not Available" values.

Data quality note:
"Not Available" should not be treated as a rating of 0. These records will need to be handled appropriately during analysis.

Overall Rating Data Quality Finding:
The dataset contains 5,419 hospitals. Of these, 2,245 (41.4%) have "Not Available" for the Hospital overall rating.

Analysis implication:
The "Not Available" records should not be treated as a rating of zero. Any analysis of overall hospital ratings should clearly account for these unavailable values.
